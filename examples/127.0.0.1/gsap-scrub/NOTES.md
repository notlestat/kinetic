# Taste note

Two shapes, two speeds of scroll. The navy square is tied to the scroll with a one-second scrub, so it trails the wheel slightly and turns a quarter as it slides 300 px: weighty, deliberate. The pale orb is locked to the scroll with no smoothing and drifts up 160 px over the whole section, which reads as a nearer layer moving at a different speed. The contrast between the lagging square and the instant orb is what makes the parallax feel physical, and the empty white section gives both room.

Could not match: the page used Lenis smooth scrolling, which the preview sandbox does not ship, so this relies on ScrollTrigger's scrub smoothing alone. State screenshots catch the square mid-scrub, so their score depends on where the page was scrolled when each was taken.
